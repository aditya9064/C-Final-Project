# Asset manifest — Fireteam: Skirmish (Godot arena)

Every asset the arena uses, where it came from, and its licence. **All third-party
content is CC0 1.0 (public domain) — no attribution legally required**; it is recorded
here for provenance. Everything else is generated by the project's own Blender scripts.
No copyrighted or ripped game assets are used.

## Downloaded — vegetation & rocks (Quaternius, CC0) — `models/nature/`
**Stylized Nature MegaKit** (free/standard), <https://opengameart.org/content/stylized-nature-megakit>,
CC0 1.0. Converted by `pipeline/convert_nature.py` to `<name>.gltf` + `<name>.bin` with one shared
`nature/textures/` folder (the kit's trees share a bark atlas, the rocks a rock atlas — stored once,
≈17 MB instead of 54 MB of embedded copies); see `nature/CREDITS.txt`.

| Model (`.gltf` + `.bin`) | Used in scene |
|---|---|
| `CommonTree_1..5` | yes — three-depth tree belt (native heights 7.0–9.4 m, rescaled) |
| `Bush_Common_Flowers` | yes — shrubs (flower surface recoloured green in-engine) |
| `Rock_Medium_1..3`, `Pebble_Round_1/3/5` | yes — boulders and pebbles |
| `Bush_Common` | converted, not placed (autumn-red atlas) |
| `Fern_1`, `Grass_Common_Tall`, `Grass_Wispy_Tall` | converted, not placed |

Shared textures: `nature/textures/Bark_NormalTree(.png|_Normal.png)`, `Leaves_NormalTree_C.png`,
`Leaves_TwistedTree_C.png`, `Flowers.png`, `Leaves.png`, `Grass.png`, `Rocks_Diffuse.png`,
`PathRocks_Diffuse.png` (all from the MegaKit, CC0). Their `.import` files are committed with
`mipmaps/generate=true`; with that setting the render is pixel-identical to the former GLBs.

## Generated — meshes (Blender 4.2 Python → GLB, project-authored) — `models/`
| GLB | Generator | Used in scene |
|---|---|---|
| `barrier_tall`, `barrier_low`, `barrier_L` | `gen_assets.py` | yes — bevelled concrete cover |
| `crate`, `case` | `gen_assets.py` | yes — crate clusters, spawn-bay cases |
| `barrel` | `gen_assets.py` | yes — barrel groups |
| `sandbag_pile` | `gen_assets.py` | yes — straight piles and curved arcs |
| `canopy`, `rock`, `spawn_arch` | `gen_assets.py` | no — superseded (canopy is now built procedurally in `build_arena.gd`) |
| `ivy` | `gen_vegetation.py` | yes — wall-top ivy |
| `tree_a`, `tree_b`, `tree_c`, `shrub` | `gen_vegetation.py` | no — superseded by the MegaKit |
| `terrain` | `gen_terrain.py` | yes — vertex-coloured ground |

Unused GLBs are kept because the generators are deterministic and the repeatability
test hashes the whole set; they cost < 1 MB in total.
