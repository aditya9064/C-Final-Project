Fireteam: Skirmish — character asset provenance
================================================

The two operative characters (male_tailored.glb = Teal team, female_tailored.glb =
Orange team) are built from CC0 (public-domain) assets by Quaternius, customized
locally with Blender (pipeline/tailor_character.py, which models real garment geometry
+ tactical gear onto the rig). No paid assets, subscriptions, or AI-generation services
were used. (Earlier pipelines assemble_character.py / dress_character.py are kept for
history; their outputs male_rigged/male_dressed/female_dressed were superseded.)

Source assets (all CC0 1.0 Universal — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/):

  Universal Base Characters  (rigged Superhero Male + Female + hairstyles)
    https://quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html
    https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-base-characters
    Downloaded: Universal Base Characters[Standard].zip  (2026-11-15, free tier)

  Universal Animation Library  (idle/walk/run/jog/jump/death + pistol aim/shoot/reload)
    https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html

License note: The live pack pages showed the CC0 badge on 2026-11-15 (download date
recorded here for provenance). No attribution is legally required under CC0; this
note is kept as good practice.

Weapons (smg_teal.glb / smg_orange.glb) are original compact carbines, generated
procedurally in Blender via pipeline/gen_weapons.py (no third-party assets): fused
two-tone receiver, red-dot optic + top rail, angled magazine, triangular skeleton
stock, muzzle brake, and team-colour accent panels, matched to the turnaround sheet.

Skeleton: all three Quaternius packs share the identical 65-bone UE/Quaternius
humanoid rig, so animations apply with no retargeting (hand_r / hand_l bones carry
the weapons). Animation clips were bundled onto each character as a single GLB.
