# Implementation summary — final environment production pass

Scope: environment only. No characters, animation, movement, weapons, AI, networking, UI or C++
gameplay were touched (`git diff --name-only HEAD` → only the pre-existing `CMakeLists.txt` edit from
before this pass; all C++ sources unchanged).

A recovery point (scripts, pipeline, project file, a tarball of `assets/models`, and the
pass-start screenshots) was saved outside the repository before any change.

## What changed (by phase)
**A — Audit.** All 8 references inspected; pass-start screenshots captured for 14+ cameras; discrepancy
report produced (`reference_comparison.md`).

**B — Hero structures** (`scripts/build_arena.gd`)
- Canopy rebuilt procedurally: 28×28 fabric grid with hip-roof profile, catenary sag inside each quadrant
  and scalloped eaves; four diagonal ridge beams (visible above and below), apex hub, perimeter eave beams,
  eave tension cable, 0.46 m posts with base plates, connection plates and bolts; four under-mounted light
  bars; deeper matte fabric (`#cf6a1a`, roughness 1.0) so sunlit top stays orange. Old `canopy.glb` no longer used.
- Portal: pillar seams added; the two flanking blocks that hid the walk-through removed.
- Banners: white diagonal stripe both faces, hem, pleats.
- Spawn gateways: team frame moved proud of the concrete, chamfered octagonal opening (corner fills),
  white diagonal stripes on jambs, white top line, concrete back wall with team/white paint splash, light
  bar in steel housing, team floor with white inset rectangle, two olive cases per bay (abstract spheres removed).

**C — Concrete & cover**
- Barriers: solid team band + steel cap replaced by a single diagonal paint stripe per face (flat polygon,
  no z-fighting); L-barriers likewise.
- New `_barrier_loophole` (2.10 m, real 1.3×0.42 m slot at 1.35 m, four collided blocks) ×2.
- Centre ring: continuous white annulus + 70° orange arc.
- Walls: warm sky-fill (0.72, warm-neutral) and warmer recessed panel so shaded faces stay beige.

**D — Props**
- Crates: olive cases re-tinted to deeper olive; new "dark" reinforced style (dark panels, steel edges,
  X-bracing) for two near-wall clusters.
- Tarp-covered pallet (wooden pallet, cargo block, draped tarp with flaps) added.
- Curved sandbag walls (3 piles on an arc) ×2 added.

**E — Natural environment**
- Trees: CC0 Quaternius MegaKit broadleaf (5 species) in three depth layers (20 near / 16 mid / 18 far),
  irregular seeded placement, heights 6.5–12 m, spawn approaches kept clear; mid/far belts cast no shadows.
- Props grounded on the sculpted terrain via `_terrain_height()` (vertex lookup of `terrain.glb`).
- Bushes: `Bush_Common_Flowers` (green atlas) with flowers recoloured; red `Bush_Common` and ghosting
  `Fern_1` not placed; footprint guard so nothing lands inside the walls.
- Terrain (`pipeline/gen_terrain.py`): muted olive/dry-grass/dirt palette; gravel apron now a soft ramp.
- Ivy: continuous overlapping bands along inner segments + denser corner clumps, natural leaf atlas.

**F — Polish.** Fabric colour, shaded-wall warmth, bush recolour, camera re-aims for honest QA views.

**G — Tests.** 17 QA renders (0 script/runtime errors, cover 5/5 each); 5-camera × 2-preset benchmarks
with frame-time stats; generator repeatability (22 protected hashes identical, `tree_b` structure intact).

**H — Review.** Final renders compared to the references; remaining differences listed in `known_issues.md`.
